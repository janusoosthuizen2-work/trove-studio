PRIVACY.

How this preview website handles contact enquiries.

Privacy information — preview version

Trove Studio's current website is a portfolio and enquiry preview. This page describes the website functionality as built. It should be reviewed and expanded with Decan before the website is publicly launched as an active business.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form uses information you type (such as your name, tattoo idea, placement and approximate size) to prepare a WhatsApp message in your browser. The website does not submit or store this information on a Trove Studio server. You decide whether to send the message once WhatsApp opens.

WhatsApp and email

If you use a WhatsApp or email contact link, your information will be handled by the service you choose. Those platforms have their own privacy practices. Decan may then use the details you send to respond to your enquiry; the artist should confirm the business's retention and contact procedures before launch.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not include analytics tracking, advertising pixels, account registration or a website contact database. Technical hosting providers may process standard connection information to serve pages.

Your questions

Contact trovestudio64@gmail.com with questions relating to information you've sent in an enquiry.

This is an operational description of a pre-launch website, not a final legal assessment. South African POPIA and other applicable legal requirements should be reviewed before collecting customer information through an expanded booking system.
